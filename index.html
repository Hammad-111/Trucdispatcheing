FAQ

Straight answers.

Do you work with owner-operators?

Yes. Our dispatch service can be structured for owner-operators and small carriers, subject to our onboarding requirements.

What equipment do you support?

We can support equipment types such as Dry Van, Reefer, and Flatbed, depending on current operations and available freight.

Do you guarantee a specific rate?

No. Freight rates vary by market, lane, equipment, timing, and other factors. We focus on finding suitable opportunities and negotiating according to the carrier's requirements.

Do you book every load you find?

No. Loads should follow the agreed carrier approval and dispatch process.

Do I have to use specific lanes?

No. Preferred lanes and operating preferences can be discussed during onboarding.

Does sending the form authorize you to book loads?

No. An inquiry is only a request for contact. Booking follows the approval process agreed after onboarding.

Who is responsible for carrier authority and insurance?

The carrier remains responsible for operating authority, insurance, safety compliance, and related obligations. Required documents are confirmed during onboarding.

Do you guarantee rates or a minimum number of loads?

No. Freight markets change. We focus on suitable opportunities and communication according to your requirements.
